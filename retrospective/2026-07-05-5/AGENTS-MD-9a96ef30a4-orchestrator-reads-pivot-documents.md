# agent instruction

**Orchestrator reads pivot documents personally.** When fanning research out to subagents, identify the small set of documents the synthesis will hinge on and read them directly instead of relying on subagent summaries; delegate breadth, never the load-bearing depth.

*Grounded in: reading k8s-platform's ai/LESSONS.md directly surfaced the doctrine contradiction that became the survey's central finding.*

# justification

The COE survey dispatched seven explorer subagents for breadth, but the orchestrator read two documents in full itself: k8s-platform's `AGENTS.md` and `ai/LESSONS.md`. That direct read is what produced the survey's headline: the precise forensic claim (152 rule candidates → 51 adopted → zero behavior change; hook-covered bug classes at zero recurrence) directly contradicts the ai-skills registry's rule-assembly design — a contradiction that had to be stated exactly to be credible, down to the wording of the remedy protocol and the amendment date. The k8s-platform survey agent's summary covered the same file accurately but at one level of compression removed; a synthesis built only on that summary would have flattened the fleet's most consequential finding into "k8s-platform prefers checks over rules." The cost of the rule is reading two or three documents (a few hundred lines each) instead of zero. The cost of skipping it is a synthesis whose central claim the author has never actually seen — unverifiable at review time and fragile under challenge.
